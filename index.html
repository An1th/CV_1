PhD Thesis

Probing Clouds and Hazes in Hot-Jupiter Atmospheres from NUV–MIR

My PhD thesis focused on the atmospheric and dynamical characterization of transiting exoplanets, particularly hot-Jupiters, using multi-wavelength transmission spectroscopy from optical to mid-infrared wavelengths.

I combined ground- and space-based observations from facilities including HST, JWST, VLT, Gemini, and TESS to investigate atmospheric chemistry, clouds, hazes, and disequilibrium processes such as vertical mixing and photochemistry.

I developed and applied forward modeling and Bayesian atmospheric retrieval techniques using POSEIDON to constrain molecular abundances, thermal structures, and aerosol properties of exoplanetary atmospheres.
